What we build

  • Scheduling & routes

    Recurring jobs scheduled from the notes you already keep, routes planned around traffic, and the day's stops on each crew phone.

    What's included
    • Recurring service built from the notes already sitting in your records
    • Routes planned around real traffic, with a cap on drive time
    • A phone screen instead of a clipboard, with the day's stops in order, working with or without signal
    • Time logged to the minute, with photos attached to the job record
    • Parts, materials, and chemicals requested from the field and tracked against the job
    • Payroll hours and mileage built from the stamps the crew already recorded
    • Nothing reaches the crew until you have looked at it and approved it
    See an example below

  • Billing & collections

    Month-end invoices drafted from the visits your crew logged, and a follow-up schedule for whatever is still unpaid.

    What's included
    • One invoice per customer drafted from the visits your crew logged, at each customer's own rate
    • Anything unusual held back for a look: a visit that ran long, extra work with no price yet, a part bought for that customer
    • Every number traced to its source: the visit, the note, the receipt photo. Visits planned but never logged are flagged before billing, so missed work turns up early
    • Proposals drafted from the site walkthrough, in good, better, and best options in your own format, priced from your price book and past jobs
    • Outstanding invoices chased on a schedule, from first nudge to final notice
    • Nothing is sent or charged until someone in your office signs off on it

  • Paperwork & reports

    Upload anything. Each document is read, filed to the right job, and the details pulled out so nobody retypes them.

    What's included
    • One place to upload everything: PDFs, scans, photos of paper forms, emailed attachments
    • Each document read, labeled, and filed to the right job, customer, or vendor
    • The details that matter (dates, totals, part numbers, signatures) pulled out, so nobody retypes them
    • Search every file in plain words instead of digging through folders
    • Reports written in your company's terms and layout, not a generic template
    • Anything the system is unsure of is flagged for a person to check

  • Customers & follow-up

    The full history on screen when they call, plus renewals, reminders, and review requests queued for you.

    What's included
    • The customer's full history on screen the moment they call
    • Replies drafted from your own records, ready to edit and send
    • Renewals, maintenance reminders, and quote follow-ups queued for you, so nobody has to remember
    • Review requests ready the day the job closes, not three weeks later
    • Campaigns built against the customers who have gone quiet
    • Referrals tracked back to the customer who sent them
    • New leads landing in the same system as the work, not a separate inbox

  • Profit & research

    See what each job really earned, ask questions in plain words, and get the numbers back.

    What's included
    • Margin by job, tech, and customer, after labor and materials, not revenue billed
    • Reports and owner briefings written for you, on whatever schedule suits you
    • Ask a question in plain words (which jobs lost money last quarter?) and get the number back instead of a report to build
    • Rebates, rule changes, and manufacturer bulletins matched to the customers they affect
    • Pricing and competitive research on the markets you are bidding into

  • Anything else

    Most of what we build isn't on this list. It starts as a description of your problem.

    What's included
    • Most of what we build is not on this list. It starts as a description of a problem.
    • Recent examples: a proposal builder that works from the site walkthrough, and month-end invoices drafted from the crew log
    • Scoped in writing and priced before any work begins
    • Built on the systems you already run, so nothing has to be torn out
    • Sized to the job, from a single workflow to the system your whole operation runs on
    • Maintained and extended after delivery, the same as everything else
