One build, as an example

The paper wall calendar
that schedules itself now

Every build starts the same way: a call about how you run today, then software shaped around that instead of over it. Nobody changes how they work to suit the tool.

For a family-owned koi-pond and water-feature contractor in the South Bay, the whole operation lived on a hand-copied wall calendar and printed route sheets. Their app reads the service notes already sitting in their QuickBooks, turns them into a real recurring schedule, and puts each day's stops on the crew's phones. Same records, same routine, minus the copying by hand.

  • In production since August 2026
  • Intuit-approved QuickBooks app, read-only
  • No new system for the crew to learn
