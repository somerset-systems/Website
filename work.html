Scheduling, dispatch and routing

1 min 46 sec, with sound and captions

Running in production since August 2026.

A pond and fountain service company planned every visit on a paper wall calendar, then copied each day by hand onto the crew's route sheet. Now the schedule builds itself from the notes they already keep in QuickBooks.

What it does

  • Reads the customer notes already in QuickBooks, like "2nd Tuesday of the month, before 10am," and fills in the calendar. When a note is unclear, it asks instead of guessing.
  • Orders each truck's day around traffic and customer time windows. The owner reviews every route before it goes out.
  • Puts the day's stops on each tech's phone. They log arrival and departure to the minute, with photos, part requests and notes from the stop.
  • Hands the office a finished crew log, printed in the same layout as the paper sheet it replaced.
  • Connects to QuickBooks read-only, through an Intuit-approved app. Nothing is written back.

Proposals from the site walkthrough

2 min 8 sec, with sound and captions

In development. Shown with a sample company and illustrative prices.

A pool remodel bid starts with a site visit and ends with someone at the office turning notes and photos into a priced proposal. This build drafts that proposal from the walkthrough itself.

What it does

  • Walks the tech through the company's own checklist on their phone. They talk or type as they go, and add photos and files like an inspection report.
  • Asks a follow-up question when something can't be priced yet, before the tech leaves, so nobody has to go back out.
  • Drafts good, better and best options in the company's own proposal format, priced from its QuickBooks price book and past jobs.
  • Shows where every number came from and flags anything it isn't sure of. The owner edits and approves before anything reaches the homeowner.
  • Shown here for pools. It can be built for any contractor who bids from a site visit.
